name: Theme direction
description: User's requested visual direction for the portfolio.
---

Use white or super-light maize as the light-mode background, maize outlines/highlights/accents to separate panels and sections, and dark gray or black text. In dark mode, use Michigan-blue accents. Avoid overwhelming blocks of solid color.

For the home page, use an Apple-website-style sans-serif font, not serif headings.

**Why:** The user said the previous large chunks of color felt overwhelming and inelegant, disliked the new serif font, and requested the Apple website font and Michigan-blue dark-mode accents.

**How to apply:** Follow this direction for theme work on the website; do not expand changes to additional pages without permission.
